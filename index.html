HOW TO USE

QUICK GUIDE

Get the most out of this app

Tap a section to open it.

🔐 Unlock & stay signed in
Enter the access code and tap Let’s go. Leave Keep me signed in on and you won’t be asked again for 30 days. Use Lock app in the menu to sign out.
📚 Practise topic by topic
Pick a subject → a topic → a set. Tap an option to see right/wrong and the full explanation instantly. The live bar at the top tracks correct, wrong and done.
🛠️ Tools on every question
☆ save · 🔊 listen · 📝 note · 🚩 report. Use the filter tabs: All, Unattempted, Wrong, Saved. The floating Back / Next / Top buttons help you move around.
⚡ Quiz, Mock & Flashcards
Quiz mode gives a timed, one-by-one test with a question palette and a result. Revise Wrong repeats your mistakes. Flashcards flip to the answer — mark Yaad hai / Nahi yaad.
🎨 Display settings
The round colour button cycles Light → Dark → AMOLED → Eye mode. Tap Aa for text size, bold and full screen.
💾 Your data
Progress, notes and saved questions live in this browser. Don’t clear site data or you’ll lose them.
